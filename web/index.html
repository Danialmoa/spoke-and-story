        Before you go
        Built for the DEV Hacktoberfest “Touch Grass” challenge. Routes from OpenStreetMap data, stories from Wikipedia, voice by Kokoro. We count anonymous usage with a rough area (about 10 km), never your exact location. With “Remember me” on, the ride buddy keeps what you tell it about your riding and the place names of your rides (never GPS) under a random ID in your browser. “Forget me” deletes all of it.
